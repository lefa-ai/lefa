Made by developers, for developers.

We run coding agents all day, and juggling them across terminals, tabs, and worktrees got old. So we're building Lefa around a simple idea: every task gets its own workspace, one you can checkpoint, fork, and leave running in the cloud after your laptop closes. We're keeping Lefa open source and model-neutral. It's still early.
